================================================================================
ISDCAS — WEBSITE VERSION ("ISDCAS-project-main copy") — CHANGE LOG
================================================================================
Baseline for this log: the original project state (the same code that is still
preserved, marked "superseded", inside the Desktop ISDCAS-project-main folder —
old general.js, old quiz.js, old single-viewport src/3d-scene.js, and
heart-basics.js). Everything below describes how the website version moved on
from that baseline.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Site navigation bar (#siteNav): brand title, inline section links on
    desktop (they drop into the ☰ drawer below ~880px), the theme toggle moved
    into the bar, a reading-progress strip along the bar's bottom edge, and
    scroll-spy highlighting of the section you are currently in (aria-current).

 2. Three new 3D models in assets/models/ (meshopt-compressed GLB with WebP
    textures — the loader now registers MeshoptDecoder to parse them):
      - beating-heart.glb   (652 KB, skinned mesh, real cardiac-cycle animation)
      - circulatory-system.glb (3.0 MB, standing figure with labels)
      - heart-lungs.glb     (4.5 MB, breathing cycle via morph targets)
    These replace the old single 15 MB heartshi.glb.

 3. Inline model slots: every section now embeds its model directly on the
    card (.model-stage / .model-slot) instead of one "View in 3D" button that
    opened a separate fullscreen viewer. The model reads as part of the page.

 4. Single shared WebGL canvas (#sceneCanvas): one fixed, transparent canvas
    for the whole page; each visible slot is drawn into its own scissor
    rectangle at the slot's current screen position every frame.

 5. Smart loading & instancing: models lazy-load via IntersectionObserver
    (200px before they reach the screen) with a per-slot progress bar; each
    model file is fetched and parsed once and cached, and slots get their own
    copy via SkeletonUtils.clone while geometry/textures stay shared.

 6. Expand-in-place: any slot can grow from its spot on the card to fill the
    screen (animated from its exact rectangle) and collapse back to exactly
    where the reader left the camera. Page scroll locks behind a dimming
    backdrop; honours prefers-reduced-motion.

 7. Expanded-mode toolbar: Reset view, Auto-rotate, Play/Pause animation,
    Labels on/off, Wireframe, Fullscreen — plus keyboard shortcuts
    (R / A / W / L / F / Space, Esc to close).

 8. Anatomical labels in the expanded view, anchored to real points on the
    mesh surface: 8 on the beating heart (aorta, superior vena cava,
    pulmonary artery, both atria, both ventricles, septum) and 7 on
    heart-lungs (larynx, trachea, bronchi, both lungs, heart, diaphragm),
    each with an info tooltip on hover. Replaces the flat hotspot image.

 9. ?pick developer mode: with ?pick in the URL, clicking a model raycasts
    the hit and logs the local-space point + surface normal in exactly the
    shape a labels entry needs — this is how the label coordinates were placed.

10. Rendering quality upgrades: ACES filmic tone mapping, image-based
    lighting (RoomEnvironment via PMREM) shared by all slots, and a
    three-point light rig per slot — the PBR materials no longer read as
    flat plastic.

11. Rewritten "Blood flow pathway" section: step-by-step pulmonary circuit,
    step-by-step systemic circuit, and a "things worth remembering" list
    (left/right rule, atrium-in/ventricle-out, thickest wall, the pulmonary
    exception, valves).

12. Hand-built SVG double-circulation diagram: six numbered stops in flow
    order, four named vessels, colour-coded oxygenated/deoxygenated paths
    with arrowheads, a legend, the heart drawn as a dashed container with
    the septum marked, colours theme-aware via CSS variables, and horizontal
    swipe-scrolling on phones instead of shrinking below legibility.

13. Per-slot titles and captions (data-title / data-caption) shown in the
    expanded viewport's header.

14. Touch behaviour designed per mode: inline slots use two-finger
    dolly-rotate and a hover-only "Drag to rotate" hint; expanded mode gets
    full one-finger rotate, two-finger pan/zoom.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REMOVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. heart-basics.js and the flat Heart-structure.jpg hotspot/tooltip diagram —
    superseded by the 3D labels in added(8). (Files are still on disk but no
    longer loaded/shown; safe to delete.)

 2. heartshi.glb usage — the old 15 MB model is no longer referenced anywhere;
    replaced by the three models in added(2). (The file itself still sits in
    assets/Model/ and can be deleted.)

 3. The old single fullscreen 3D viewer flow: the per-card "View in 3D"
    launch buttons, the #canvas-holder renderer, and the hardcoded model
    transform (scale 2,2,2 / position 0,-15,0). Framing is now measured
    automatically from the model's real bounds.

 4. The free-floating fixed theme-toggle and menu buttons — their jobs moved
    into the nav bar (added(1)).

 5. Inline-style show/hide of the chrome from general.js (style.display
    writes on the buttons) — replaced by classes on <body>.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ERRORS FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Fullscreen expand broke when the card was mid-hover: the card lifts on
    hover (a transform), which silently becomes the containing block for
    position:fixed — so the slot grew toward the CARD's bounds instead of the
    screen's, then snapped when the hover ended. Fixed by hoisting the slot
    to <body> for the duration of the expand.  [found during added(6)]

 2. One WebGL context per model would hit the browser's context cap (~8–16;
    oldest contexts get dropped). Fixed by the single shared scissored
    canvas.  [found during added(3)/(4)]

 3. Camera ended up INSIDE the beating heart: the model is authored at 1/100
    scale and blown back up by its bones, so the naive bind-pose bounding box
    came out ~0.02 units across. Fixed by using SkinnedMesh.computeBoundingBox
    after updateMatrixWorld (which refreshes bindMatrixInverse — the
    similar-sounding updateWorldMatrix does not).  [found during added(2)]

 4. The heart's bottom was cropped every contraction when framed to its
    resting pose only. Fixed by sampling 8 poses across the animation clip
    and fitting to the union of them. Related: the skinned mesh's stale
    bounding sphere could cull the whole model mid-beat — frustum culling is
    disabled for skinned meshes.  [found during added(2)]

 5. Every copy of a skinned model beat in lockstep with whichever was updated
    last: plain Object3D.clone copies bones but leaves all copies bound to
    the original skeleton. Fixed with SkeletonUtils.clone.
    [found during added(5)]

 6. The Expand button was unclickable: OrbitControls calls setPointerCapture
    on pointerdown, redirecting the rest of the gesture to the slot so the
    click resolved onto the slot, never the button. Fixed by stopping
    pointerdown propagation on the button.  [found during added(6)/(7)]

 7. Inline models hijacked page scrolling on touch: OrbitControls sets
    touch-action:none when it connects. Fixed by re-applying
    touch-action:pan-y (vertical swipes scroll the page, sideways drags
    rotate the model) and disabling wheel-zoom/pan while inline.
    [found during added(3)]

 8. Locking the page scroll shifted the whole layout sideways: overflow:hidden
    removes the scrollbar, widening the viewport — very visible against a
    model mid-animation. Fixed by adding the scrollbar's width back as body
    padding while locked.  [found during added(6)]

 9. The circulatory model's camera was pushed miles back: the export carries
    stray label geometry scattered far from the body, ballooning the bounding
    box. Fixed by hiding anything sitting well outside the focus node, plus
    one named dud ("LabelGroup4012" — exported with zero Y scale, drew as an
    empty grey pill next to the heart).  [found during added(2)]

10. The beating heart rendered as flat washed-out pink: exported with a
    full-strength emissive map fighting our lighting. Fixed by dialling
    emissiveIntensity down to 0.25.  [found during added(2)/(10)]

11. The heart-lungs model clipped its frame while "breathing": bounds
    measurement can't see morph-target deformation, so its fit uses extra
    padding (fitOffset 1.14) for the model to breathe into.
    [found during added(2)]

12. The old free-floating theme toggle collided with page content — solved
    by moving it into the nav bar.  [found during added(1)]

13. Intro dismissal stranded chrome on screen: the old inline style.display
    writes would win against the media queries that swap the nav links and
    the ☰ drawer at different widths. Fixed by driving it with a class on
    <body> instead.  [found during added(1)]

14. The drawer stayed open covering the section it had just jumped to —
    it now closes itself when a link inside it is clicked.
    [found during added(1)]

15. Scroll-spy highlighted the WRONG (previous) section after clicking a nav
    link: an anchor jump parks the section where scroll-margin-top says, which
    could sit just short of the highlight line. The line now reads
    scroll-margin-top off the element itself so the two always agree; also a
    divide-by-zero guard for pages shorter than the window (treated as 100%
    progress), and a bottom-of-page override so the last section highlights.
    [found during added(1)]

16. Collapse animated the model to a position that no longer existed: the
    target rectangle was measured before the scroll unlock re-flowed the
    page. It is now measured after unlocking — and against the stage element,
    since the slot is still parented to <body> at that moment.
    [found during added(6)]

17. Clones inherited stale world matrices (GLTFLoader hands the scene back
    without computing them) — settled once with updateMatrixWorld(true) at
    load so every clone starts correct.  [found during added(5)]

18. Labels for anatomy on the far side of the model read as if they belonged
    to whatever was in front. They now dim (not hide) based on the surface
    normal recorded at placement time — normals, because a chamber wall seen
    through a cutaway faces the camera even though it sits behind the model's
    centre, so a simple depth test would wrongly dim it. Labels fully behind
    the camera are hidden so the projection can't mirror them back into view.
    [found during added(8)]
